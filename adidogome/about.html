Deborah Tsogbe is a cultural practitioner most interested in mysticism, power, and identity, and their exploration through systems thinking & computational means. Their work most often manifests in textile work (which is fundamentally computational), writing, and soft systems. All of these material means aim to marry the fuzziness of reality with the regimented boundaries of systems architectures. Deborah holds a B.Arch from University of Southern California and an M.S in Computational Design from Massachusetts Institute of Technology.
