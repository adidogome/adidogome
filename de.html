déborah

i am building this space to hold documentation for my works and dreams

(i, afy déborah lauren tsogbé)

you may find the specificity of name unnecessary, but it's certain that the déborah tsogbé referred to in this space is not the only one in the world.

i am most interested in memory, mysticism, power, and relationships, and how they can be explored through crafty and computational means. recently, this tends to manifest in textile work (which is fundamentally computational) and writing. it can also be recognized in soft systems, archival work, and physical objects or spaces. these material means want to marry the fuzziness of reality with the regimented boundaries of systems architectures.
