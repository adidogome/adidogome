environmental surveillance in giant territory

this is an inquiry into weather and disaster monitoring networks in and around the sequoias. giant territory is the most peaceful place i have ever been. it is also where i have taken some of my deepest breaths. when i die i would like to be buried in the seed pod of a sequoia. before i die, i would like to know who is watching the trees. and why.

-- military environmental complex --

the first park rangers in the USA were soldiers. Yosemite, Sequoia, and General Grant (Kings Canyon) national parks were established 25 years before the creation of the national park service and long before the average American understood the need to tread carefully in the wildlands. so, for years, US Army soldiers preserved, patrolled and policed the Sierras, protecting parks from visitors and passers-through.

-- agentic nature --

'nature' is a broad, flexible concept. on one hand pure and innocent, somewhere to escape to; on the other brutal and unforgiving, a place where violence prevails. humans are considered both apart from and within nature. what we create (technology et al) is prescribed 'unnatural' and yet we set out to invent things that observe, mimic and reflect the same nature we disocciate ourselves from.
