inconnu 1

i started doing these more as a grounding exercise. i use copper wire as my line because it feels natural, given what i've learned about woven memory and further, about textile fabrication as the genesis of computers as we know them. braiding, twisting, and threading copper integrates craft sets from compartments of my life i have been trying to marry for a bit. the act of mapping physical and dreamt territories helps me work through some of the shadowier areas of my psyche. each landscape i travel through is an opportunity for deep reflection.
